07 / QUESTIONS

Curious minds welcome.

New to MUN? Here's what you need to know before taking the first step.

I've never attended an MUN. Can I join?

Absolutely. Our workshop explains MUN fundamentals, committee procedures, speaking and resolution writing for first-time delegates.

What is Model United Nations?

It's an educational simulation in which students represent countries or viewpoints, debate issues, negotiate and collaborate on solutions.

Is Qawali Night included?

No. Qawali Night passes are sold separately from the registration package.

Where will the conference be held?

Best Western Premier Hotel, Quetta, Balochistan. Transportation to and from BUMHS is included.

What do I get as a delegate?

Two conference days, introductory workshop, specified transportation, dinners, two included social events, refreshments, delegate materials, chair guidance, certificates and networking opportunities.

How can I apply as a delegation?

Use the official Delegation Registration tab above to access the dedicated form.
